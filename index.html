Host record

Each mapped point represents one host record. Parasite and geographic filters retain hosts with at least one linked parasite record matching the active criteria.

Geographic provenance

Country, state/province, locality, coordinates, and coordinate uncertainty in this release are derived from locality metadata associated with linked parasite specimen records. They are not independently retrieved authoritative host metadata. Future releases will join host GUIDs directly to authoritative host records.

Data interpretation

Museum records reflect collecting effort, geographic and temporal sampling, digitization, and taxonomic identification. Record density is not a direct measure of abundance or prevalence, and absence of records is not evidence of biological absence.
